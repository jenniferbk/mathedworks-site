M2Studio mathematical modeling environment and module

A web workspace where high school students build, test and present mathematical models, with a 10-hour modeling module for Algebra I, Algebra II and Integrated Math.

  • Grades 9–12
  • Free with registration
  • Device per student, Internet, Specific software

Get the materialslearn.concord.org

Before you use it

  • You need a teacher account on learn.concord.org to see the module materials; registration asks for an email verification. Students also make accounts (Google or Schoology sign-in works) and join your class with a class word. The portal's About page says its activities are free and registration is free.
  • Students' responses in the portal are automatically logged and anonymized for the project's research. The developers ask you to share an information sheet with students and families before you start. The project also asks for help collecting research and evaluation data, but its sources disagree: the module page says this is data you "will need to collect," while the research-data document says you "can also help." Ask the project (m2studio@concord.org) what is expected.
  • The materials have been revised since the pilot: the module was piloted in a few classrooms in 2023–24, and the older beta version is still posted beside the current one. The developers recommend doing the lessons in order.
  • Plan about 10 hours for the module, for Algebra I, Algebra II or Integrated Math. Lesson-by-lesson timing was not available to us without an account.

What a teacher gets

M2Studio is the Concord Consortium's web-based learning environment for mathematical modeling, built on its CODAP and SageModeler tools. Students work in tiles: a text tile for notes, a sketch tile for drawings and uploaded images, and a diagram tile where they connect variable cards with expressions to build a model. Variables carry values and units, standard units convert automatically, validation tools check expressions and units, and students can test a model with different inputs and publish their work for the class to see.

The developers' 10-hour module for Algebra I, Algebra II and Integrated Math classes includes modeling lessons, checks for understanding, pre- and post-assessments, and teacher slides, guides and handouts. A standards document (April 2024) names lessons on time in school and salary, food waste and Kenia's challenge, and maps them to high school algebra, function and quantity standards. Through the learn.concord.org portal, teachers set up a class, assign lessons, view students' workspaces in near real time and play back each student's work.

How these awards connect to this product

The Concord Consortium's M2Studio project page states "This material is based upon work supported by the National Science Foundation under Grant No. DRL-2101382." The NSF record (Chao PI; Finzer, Zbiek, Galluzzo and Ellis co-PIs; 2021–2027) is titled for M2Studio and describes building it by integrating CODAP and SageModeler into one workspace. CODAP's own NSF awards are listed on the CODAP card; SageModeler's funding is not covered here. The award is still active, and the module page says the materials were piloted in 2023–24 and revised since.
