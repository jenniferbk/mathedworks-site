Before you use it

  • Price: the publisher's store lists a "Pathways to Calculus" workbook and online text (11th edition) at $96.95, but the pages we saved do not say this is the precalculus course, so confirm the match before buying. The same order page gives the access length as both 300 and 200 days. School pricing isn't posted; ask through the contact form.
  • Adoption comes with professional development from the Pathways team, starting with two- or three-day workshops at your site. The publisher presents materials and PD as a package.
  • Students need internet access for the online lessons, practice and assessments; the workbook itself is print.
  • Your course may not match the default table of contents: the developers customize courses for each site, and modules 9-11 (polar coordinates, vectors, sequences and series) are online or in custom printings only.
  • The store lists both a 10th and an 11th edition; the publisher has revised the materials continually since piloting began in 2006.

Try one lesson

Module 3: Investigation 1, The Box Problem and Modeling Relationships pathwaysmath.com

  • High school and college
  • Modeling relationships between covarying quantities
  • Time not stated
  • Free, no login

Students build an open-top box by cutting equal squares from the corners of an 8.5-by-11-inch sheet, complete a table relating the cutout length to the base and volume, graph volume against cutout length, and use a graphing calculator to find cutout lengths that give a volume of 62.5 cubic inches.

You need: Sheets of 8.5-by-11-inch paper, scissors, tape and graphing calculators.

This is the publisher's sample instructor guide: instructor notes with the student problems, solutions and teaching advice.

What a teacher gets

Pathways Precalculus is a high school and college precalculus course organized around how quantities change together and around rate of change, which the developers present as preparation for calculus. The default course has eleven modules, from symbolic reasoning and quantitative relationships through functions, exponential and logarithmic, polynomial and power, and rational functions, to trigonometry; the last three modules (polar coordinates, vectors, and sequences and series) are online or in custom printings. The developers say it has been used in both honors and non-honors classes.

Students work in a consumable workbook with daily lesson investigations and homework. Online resources add lessons linked to each investigation, applets, repeatable practice, short videos and assessments with immediate feedback. Instructor guides give learning goals, solutions, lesson-planning advice and common student errors; the publisher also lists PowerPoints for each investigation and assessment item banks. Rational Reasoning also offers Algebra I, Algebra II and college algebra courses, and its Geometry course is still being developed.

How these awards connect to this product

The publisher says Pathways research has been funded by two NSF grants but does not give award numbers. The two listed are Carlson's Math and Science Partnership awards at Arizona State University. The record for 1050721 says it builds on Project Pathways, a targeted MSP whose Phase I produced the Pathways Precalculus Professional Development Model, including in-class student activities with detailed teacher notes, and that 1050721 studies implementation of Pathways Precalculus materials. That record gives no award number for Project Pathways; we identify it as 0412537 because that award is titled Project Pathways and is a targeted MSP award to Carlson at Arizona State. We infer these are the two grants the site means, but the site does not say so. Thompson's precalculus professional learning community award (0353470), Carlson's CAREER award (9876127) and a graduate-student teaching award (1323753) were left off because their records do not describe the curriculum. The record for 0412537 does not mention a precalculus curriculum, and no source names the awards, so the connection is recorded as inferred.
