What a teacher gets

Project MATHEMATICS! is a series of computer-animated videos created by Tom Apostol at Caltech on basic topics in high school mathematics. Caltech's YouTube playlist holds 14 videos: nine episodes of about 19 to 28 minutes, plus three short animation trailers, a teachers workshop video and a contest video.

The episodes are Similarity (scaling and its effect on perimeter, area and volume), The Story of Pi, The Theorem of Pythagoras (several animated proofs, Pythagorean triples), three parts on Sines and Cosines (waves and the unit circle; the laws of cosines and sines with the Great Survey of India; addition formulas), Polynomials (translating and scaling graphs, zeros and extrema), The Tunnel of Samos (similar triangles and an ancient tunnel) and Early History of Mathematics. Each episode's description says a Program Guide and Workbook exists for every episode except 6 and 9, and links a Caltech Box folder that lists PDF and TIFF files for episodes 1–5, 7 and 8. The first NSF award planned each tape in short numbered segments keyed to the workbooks, so a teacher could stop for discussion.

How these awards connect to this product

Two NSF awards to Tom Apostol at Caltech name the product. 8850730 ("Project MATHEMATICS|", 1989–1991) was to produce six computer-animated modules on high school geometry, each with student workbooks and teacher manuals. 9150082 ("Project Mathematics", 1991–1996) was to produce tapes for high school students, each with a workbook. A third award, 9553580 ("Interactive Project MATHEMATICS!", 1996–2000), funded CD-ROM versions, not the videos, but its abstract says the videotapes were "developed at California Institute of Technology under two previous NSF grants." The abstract does not name those two grants; that they are 8850730 and 9150082 is our inference from the award titles, PI and dates. Caltech's YouTube descriptions date the episodes from ©1988 (Episode 3, The Theorem of Pythagoras) to ©2000 (Episode 9, Early History). The 8850730 record says the team had already made a pilot tape of "The Theorem of Pythagoras" before that award, and Episode 9 is dated after 9150082 ended, so no source ties every episode to these awards. We use "Funded part of this product." 9452762 (1994–1995) was a CD-ROM planning grant and is not listed.
