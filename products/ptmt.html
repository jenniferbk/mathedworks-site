Before you use it

  • These are course materials for teacher educators and the prospective middle and secondary teachers they teach, not lessons for grade 6-12 students: teachers work the tasks as learners, then analyze video of students' thinking.
  • Free. Chapter documents, readings and solutions are Google Drive files linked from the project site; the Teaching Algebra, Teaching Geometry and Teaching Data Analysis and Probability pages also have a "Download All Materials" link.
  • Check the software first. Teaching Geometry is built on The Geometer's Sketchpad and Teaching Data Analysis and Probability on TinkerPlots and Fathom. Teaching Statistics Investigations redoes the first four DAP chapters with the free online CODAP, and Teaching Algebra uses GeoGebra.
  • Teaching Algebra, Teaching Geometry and Teaching Data Analysis and Probability come with Common Core alignment documents. The Guiding Framework is copyright 2015, and time per chapter is not stated on the pages we saved.
  • The LEAD-DG geometry discussion modules are incomplete. On Oct 30, 2026 the overview materials and Module 6 were marked "coming soon".

What a teacher gets

PTMT, an NSF-funded project running since 2005, posts free materials for mathematics teacher education on using technology to teach middle and secondary algebra, statistics and geometry. Collections are meant to be used together, though many teacher educators combine materials across collections to build a course. Teaching Algebra (7 chapters, GeoGebra and online applets), Teaching Geometry (7 chapters, The Geometer's Sketchpad), Teaching Data Analysis and Probability (6 chapters, TinkerPlots, Fathom, Excel and graphing calculators) and Teaching Statistics Investigations (4 chapters, the free online tool CODAP) engage teachers as learners of the mathematics while building technology skills and teaching strategies; several chapters include video of students working with the software. The algebra chapter page we checked links a chapter document, video transcripts, technology files, teaching tips, suggested readings and sample solutions.

Examining Students' Practices in Algebra and Function adds 8 video-based modules on noticing student thinking and the 5 Practices for orchestrating discussions; Module 2, the one we checked, links instructor materials for all five tasks and class materials for four. A set of geometry discussion modules (LEAD-DG) is still being posted.

Who made it

Preparing to Teach Mathematics with Technology (PTMT). Developed by Hollylynne Lee, Karen Hollebrands, Allison McCulloch, Jennifer Lovett, Charity Cayton and Kayla Chandler.

How these awards connect to this product

The project page credits DUE 0042319, 0817253, 1123001 and 1820976 (NC State), 1820998 (Middle Tennessee State), 1821054 (UNC Charlotte) and 1820967 (East Carolina). The NSF API has no record for 0042319. The PTMT Guiding Framework credits "DUE 04-42319" instead, and the project's About Us page shows the text "0042319" linked to NSF award 0442319. That award (Lee and Hollebrands, NC State, 2005-2007) is a proof-of-concept project to build a prototype Data Analysis and Probability module for prospective teachers, so we list 0442319 and read 0042319 as a typo. The About Us page also links further awards to project members. We list 1625713 (ESTEEM, Lee) because the Teaching Statistics Investigations page says those chapters were developed as part of the ESTEEM project, and 2235338 (Hollebrands, NC State) and 2235393 (Chandler, East Carolina), the two halves of a collaborative award to build online modules on leading geometry discussions with web-based dynamic geometry, because the About Us page names them as LEAD-DG project awards. We leave out 1712280, which funded the Forging Connections Between Geometry and Functions materials that PTMT links on an external site this card does not describe, and 1954692, a workshop award. All listed awards are confirmed in the API.
