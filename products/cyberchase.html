Cyberchase (PBS KIDS)

Free PBS LearningMedia collection built on the Cyberchase math series: 136 video clips and 68 lesson plans for grades K–8, plus games on PBS KIDS.

  • Grades K–8
  • Free, no login
  • Projector, Internet
  • Item bank, Video

Get the materialspbslearningmedia.org

Before you use it

  • Free to read without an account; a free PBS LearningMedia account adds saving, state standards, class assignments and Google Classroom sync.
  • The collection is mostly short video clips; lesson plans are about a third of the items. The lesson we read links its clip as QuickTime Video; its date is not stated.
  • Cyberchase also covers environmental themes (one game, for example, has players power rides with renewable energy), and PBS keeps a separate Cyberchase: Environmental Science collection; the math collection is the one described here.
  • PBS describes the TV series as developed for kids ages 8 to 11 (its About page also says 6 to 8 in one place); the collection's items run from K–2 to 6–8. Games and family activities are on PBS KIDS and PBS Parents.
  • The three NSF awards listed ran from 2000 to 2010. The collection credits current funders and says past major funders include the National Science Foundation; which clips come from NSF-funded seasons is not stated.

Try one lesson

Planning a Budget | Cyberchase pbslearningmedia.org

  • Grades 3–5
  • Column addition and lining up decimal points in the context of money
  • Time not stated
  • Free, no login

Students watch the clip An Unexpected Guest, in which Harry's $100 budget for a date changes when his cousin Harley joins, and record the meal and dessert costs on a handout to find how much Harry has left. Assessments add column-addition practice and a misaligned decimal addition to troubleshoot.

You need: Handout 1, "A Date for Three", and a way to play the video clip for the class (twice, if needed).

What a teacher gets

Cyberchase is a public-television animated series in which three kids and their cyberpal Digit use math to stop the villain Hacker; live-action "For Real" segments show the math in everyday life. For teachers, PBS LearningMedia's free "Cyberchase: Math" collection lists Video (136), Lesson Plan (68), Media Gallery (4), Document (1) and Interactive (1) for grades K–2, 3–5 and 6–8, grouped under Algebra, Data Analysis and Measurement, Geometry, Numbers and Operations, and Math and Health.

We read one lesson plan, "Planning a Budget" (grades 3–5). Students practice column addition and lining up decimal points with money: the teacher reads a scenario, hands out a budget sheet, plays a clip in which Harry budgets $100 for a date, and discusses the answers. Two assessments follow, one at proficiency (adding charity contributions) and one above proficiency (diagnosing an incorrect addition).

How these awards connect to this product

All three NSF records (Sheppard, Educational Broadcasting Corporation) are titled Cyberchase and fund production of the series and its web and outreach materials; 9909404 covers 13 half-hour episodes, web activities and a teacher's guide, 0307763 the third season (its record lists planned TV episodes on topics such as percent, mixed-number fractions, tessellations, ratio and proportion, experimental probability and stem-and-leaf plots; no source ties these episodes to items in the collection), and 0741683 seven new episodes and the "Do the Math!" video shorts. The shortlist group lists five more Cyberchase awards from 1998 to 2010 (9725610, 0206195, 0407065, 0540279, 1010981), not repeated here. The PBS LearningMedia collection names current funders and says past major funders include the National Science Foundation; no source we saved says which clips or lesson plans came from the NSF-funded seasons.
