What a teacher gets

Math Expressions is a core mathematics program for PreK through grade 6, now sold by Heinemann (a division of HMH). Its author, Karen Fuson of Northwestern University, based it on her Children's Math Worlds research project, which Heinemann describes as decade-long and NSF-funded. The program is organized around five classroom structures from that research: Building Concepts, Math Talk, Student Leaders, Quick Practice and Helping Community. Students work with manipulatives and math boards and explain their reasoning to one another.

For K–6, Heinemann lists print Teacher Editions, Student Activity Books, a manipulative kit, math boards and an Activity Center for differentiation, plus downloadable assessment guides, Homework and Remembering pages, practice, reteach and challenge pages, and RTI Tier 3 materials. Digital tools on the Heinemann Flight platform include eBooks, virtual manipulatives, digital assessments and reports, and Matific adaptive practice. Assessments include beginning-, middle- and end-of-year tests. Many components come in Spanish.

How these awards connect to this product

Heinemann says Math Expressions is based on the curriculum developed by Karen Fuson's decade-long, NSF-funded Children's Math Worlds (CMW) Research Project, but gives no award numbers. Award 9816320 is titled "Children's Math Worlds" in the NSF record and funded K–3 materials development, so we list it. Fuson's other NSF awards from the same years (9353573 and 9806020, on classroom supports for children's arithmetic in English- and Spanish-speaking classrooms) do not name CMW or the curriculum in their records, so we leave them off; they may be part of the decade of research Heinemann describes.
