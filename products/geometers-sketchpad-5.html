The Geometer's Sketchpad 5

Retired desktop dynamic-geometry software for Mac and Windows; no longer sold, but a 5.1 field-test edition can be downloaded under a field-test agreement; no price is stated.

  • Grades 3–12
  • Agreement or training required
  • Specific software
  • Framework or toolkit, Software

Get the materialsdynamicgeometry.com

Before you use it

  • You can't buy it. McGraw Hill retired Sketchpad as of July 2019 and ended support and short-term access after June 30, 2020. Schools with existing licenses may keep using them, but no updates are planned and McGraw Hill warns functionality could decay.
  • Without a license, version 5 runs only in Preview mode: 20-minute sessions, no saving, printing or copying, and teachers are not authorized to use it in the classroom.
  • The field-test site offers 5.1 builds to "existing Sketchpad customers and other field-testers". Its usage agreement lets field testers, and the students and colleagues they allow, use it in ways that support the field-test evaluation. It is beta, unsupported and may have bugs, and whether it works fully without a license code is not stated. We have not tested it.
  • The installers we saw are for Mac and Windows computers.
  • It is a tool, not lessons. Activities come separately, from the download center's collections and the free Dynamic Number activities.

Try one lesson

Parallel Pairs: Parallelogram and Triangle Area web.archive.org

  • Grades 6–8
  • Parallelogram and triangle area
  • About 45 minutes
  • Free, no login

Students construct a parallelogram in Sketchpad, shear it and change its base and height to see which moves change the area, and write an area formula. They then construct a triangle with the same base and height and write a formula for its area.

You need: Computers with The Geometer's Sketchpad 5 and a copy of the worksheet for each student.

The download center's link to these files no longer returns them, so this links a 2016 archived copy. Sketchpad was retired in 2019, and without a license it runs only in Preview mode, which teachers are not authorized to use in class.

What a teacher gets

The Geometer's Sketchpad is desktop software for constructing geometric figures and then dragging them while the construction's relationships hold, so students can make and test conjectures across many cases. It performs transformations and measures lengths, angles and areas. Its download center describes it as for students from third grade through college.

McGraw Hill retired Sketchpad as of July 2019. The dynamicgeometry.com download center still links version 5.06 installers and activity collections for grades 3–5 and 6–8, algebra, geometry, precalculus and calculus, plus the Dynamic Number project's 70 free Sketchpad activities for grades 2–11. A separate field-test site offers version 5.1 builds for current macOS (including Apple Silicon) and Windows 11 that can also export sketches as Web Sketchpad pages.

Who made it

Visual Geometry Project (Swarthmore College); later Key Curriculum Press / KCP Technologies. Developed by Eugene A. Klotz.

How these awards connect to this product

Both awards are Eugene Klotz's Visual Geometry projects at Swarthmore, mainly for videotapes and workbooks, with the program as a component. The 1986 record (8550459) says a "geometer's sketch pad" program would be a byproduct of the project; the 1989 record (8850726) describes The Geometer's Sketchpad as a two-dimensional program for constructing and manipulating geometric figures. Version 5 was developed commercially later; no source we saved says NSF funded version 5.
