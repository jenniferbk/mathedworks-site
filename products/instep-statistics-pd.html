InSTEP (Invigorating Statistics Teacher Education through Professional Online Learning)

Free, self-paced online professional learning for grades 6–12 teachers and coaches on teaching statistics and data science, organized by seven teaching dimensions.

  • Grades 6–12
  • Free with registration

Get the materialsinstepwithdata.org

Before you use it

  • Free, but you must create an account and accept the platform's terms; the Friday Institute may use your contact information to send related resources and research or PD opportunities.
  • Self-paced and online; you need a computer and internet access. The roller coaster lesson in the preview uses CODAP, a free browser-based data tool.
  • The modules and their length are behind the login; the public preview offers a few samples, of which we read two, and the time needed to earn a certificate is not stated on the public pages.
  • This is professional learning for teachers, not a student curriculum. The one classroom lesson in the public preview (two 60-minute periods, 2–3 students per laptop) is an ESTEEM lesson; we could not see whether InSTEP's own modules include lessons.
  • The NSF award that built InSTEP ran through April 2026; the project page describes 2019–2023 goals. The site was live on Oct 25, 2026, but no source says how long it will be maintained.

What a teacher gets

InSTEP is a free online professional learning platform for teachers and instructional coaches who teach statistics and data science in grades 6–12, built by NC State's Friday Institute and run with RTI International. Teachers choose their own path through two kinds of material: hands-on data investigations, which use multivariate real-world data and visualization tools, and modules grouped under seven dimensions of teaching (data and statistical practices, central statistical ideas, tasks, data, technology tools, argumentation and discourse, and assessment of student thinking). The platform tracks progress, lets teachers save resources and talk with other members, and offers certificates; RTI says educators can receive Continuing Education Units.

We could see only the public preview. One sample, a task-sort activity in the tasks dimension, asks teachers to choose among five pairs of classroom tasks and compare their opportunities for statistical reasoning, in about 30 minutes. The preview also includes a two-period lesson plan for grades 6–12 in which students explore roller coaster data in CODAP; the plan credits it to the ESTEEM project (NSF DUE 1625713), not to InSTEP.

How these awards connect to this product

The NC State project page says InSTEP is supported by NSF grant DRL 1908760, and the NSF record for 1908760 (Lee, NC State, 2019) describes the online professional learning environment for grades 6–12 statistics and data science teachers. The roller coaster lesson plan we read credits the ESTEEM project (NSF DUE 1625713); that award is not listed here because no source ties it to the InSTEP platform itself.
