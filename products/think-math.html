Think Math! and Math+C

EDC's NSF-funded K–5 curriculum, whose free student pages are gone from EDC's site and, as far as we found, survive only in web archives, plus free Snap! microworlds for grades 2–5.

  • Grades K–5
  • Free, no login

Before you use it

  • The free Think Math! PDFs are no longer on EDC's site (download links return "not found", checked Oct 25, 2026). You can get archived copies of the student pages from the Wayback Machine; the archive link here is the grade 3 listing.
  • You'd be teaching without a teacher guide: EDC's site describes guides but we found none to download there, live or archived. The Activity Masters are labelled as part of a Teacher Resource Book.
  • The grade 3 chapter 1 pages we opened cite NCTM standards, not the Common Core; their page footers carry print-production dates from November and December 2006.
  • Math+C microworlds run in a web browser (live when checked Oct 25, 2026). The Fractions on a Number Line and Arrays pages each say the microworld takes less than 10 minutes to introduce; EDC recommends starting with Number Line and notes that Arrays is harder because block order matters.
  • Free to use: the site footer applies a Creative Commons BY-NC-ND 4.0 license, though the PDFs themselves carry only an EDC copyright line.

Try one lesson

Fractions on a Number Line go.edc.org

  • Grades 2–5
  • Adding and subtracting on the number line
  • Time not stated
  • Free, no login

Students snap programming blocks together to move a circle along a number line, first in whole numbers and then on a "zoomed in" line in eighths, solving puzzles that add and subtract fractions; later challenges use halves and mixed numbers. Nothing is checked: they see what their code does and change it.

You need: A device with a web browser.

What a teacher gets

Think Math! is a K–5 curriculum from Education Development Center (EDC) that builds skills through puzzles, investigations and games. EDC's site describes daily Lesson Activity Book (LAB) pages that children work on near the end of each lesson, practice and extension pages, blackline Activity Masters, a kindergarten flip chart and a Student Handbook for grades 3–5, plus daily routines such as Headline Stories and Mental Math. In 2023 the site offered all student materials free as PDFs by chapter (15 chapters in grade 3). Those links now return "not found", but the Internet Archive indexes student PDFs for several grades; we opened grade 3, chapter 1. We found no teacher guides to download, live or archived. Think Math! was also published commercially in print (a 2006 citation names School Specialty Math).

Math+C is EDC's newer work: programming microworlds in the block language Snap!, in mini-units for grades 2–5. Each microworld page gives a purpose, a description of the puzzles, how to introduce it and teacher notes. Examples include Number Line, Arrays, Fractions on a Number Line and Coordinates.

How these awards connect to this product

EDC's Think Math! page says development of Think Math! was supported under ESI-0099093. Awards 1741792 and 1934161 funded only the Math+C programming add-on: EDC's programming page says the Snap! mini-units are being developed under those two grants. The site footer labels 1934161 as "Think Math+C" and 1741792 as "Math+C"; the NSF records and EDC's Authors & Advisors page have it the other way round (1741792 is "Think Math + C", 1934161 is "Mathematics through Programming in the Elementary Grades"). We follow the NSF records. The NSF record for 0099093 ("Learning By Doing: A Mathematics Curriculum for Elementary School Children and Their Teachers") does not name Think Math! in its title or abstract; the link rests on EDC's statement.
