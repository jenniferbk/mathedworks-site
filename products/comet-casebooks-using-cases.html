What a teacher gets

Volume 1 of a three-volume set by Margaret Schwan Smith, Edward A. Silver and Mary Kay Stein (Teachers College Press, 2005). Volume 2 covers algebra and Volume 3 geometry and measurement. Each volume is built from cases developed under the NSF-funded COMET program: each case shows an instructional episode in an urban middle-school classroom where a teacher uses a cognitively challenging task, told from the teacher's perspective. Each volume also has facilitation chapters for professional developers, with guidelines for leading discussions and connecting the cases to teachers' own practice.

We did not see Volume 1 itself, so we can't list its cases. A Mathematics Education Leadership review of Volume 3 describes four cases there, each with an opening activity, the case, analysis questions and activities for connecting to your own practice, plus appendices of sample responses; we have not confirmed that Volume 1 is organized the same way. The books are written for teachers of mathematics and the professional developers who work with them.

How these awards connect to this product

The tie is the publisher's wording: the series uses 'materials that were developed under the NSF-funded COMET (Cases of Mathematics to Enhance Teaching) program'. We have not seen the book's front matter, so we can't say NSF paid for the book itself. The publisher's description says the series uses materials developed under the NSF-funded COMET program. 9731428 is the COMET award in the NSF API (1998-2002; Smith as PI, Silver and Stein as co-PIs), which describes sixteen middle-school cases in four clusters, one of them on rates, ratios and proportions. The award record says the cases draw on QUASAR project materials. The shortlist's other awards for this group (8019328, 0101799, 0301962, 0732798) are not tied to these books by any source we found and are left off.
