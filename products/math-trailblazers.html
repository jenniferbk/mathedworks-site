What a teacher gets

Math Trailblazers is a K-5 mathematics curriculum published by Kendall Hunt, now in its 4th edition. The publisher describes it as inquiry-based, reflecting NCTM's Principles and Standards and aligned with the Common Core, with hands-on activities, discussion and cooperative learning, and says the 4th edition is designed to treat the teacher as a learner. It grew out of the TIMS Project, whose NSF-funded curriculum integrated mathematics with science experiments.

The publisher's grade-by-grade description runs from whole numbers, sets and shapes in kindergarten, through addition and subtraction strategies and place value in grades 1-2, multiplication, division and fractions in grades 3-4, to fraction operations, ratios and decimals in grade 5. A homeschool teacher quoted on the publisher's page mentions activities, games and labs. We could not see the teacher or student materials, so we cannot describe lesson structure, assessments or pacing.

Who made it

Teaching Integrated Mathematics and Science (TIMS) Project, University of Illinois at Chicago. Developed by Philip D. Wagreich, Howard S. Goldberg and Catherine Kelso.

How these awards connect to this product

Kendall Hunt says Math Trailblazers is based on more than 25 years of NSF-funded research by the TIMS Project at the University of Illinois at Chicago but gives no award numbers. The NSF record for 0242704 says Math Trailblazers was developed with NSF funds through the TIMS project and funds a study of its use and a revision of the curriculum (2003-2011); 9050226 (1990-1997) funded development of a complete K-6 curriculum integrating mathematics and science based on TIMS materials. No source says whether the 0242704 revision is the 4th edition sold now, so the relation is recorded as an earlier edition. Wagreich's 8850466 (calculator materials for grades 4-8) and 9550068 (school-university partnerships) are not listed because their records do not describe this curriculum.
