Before you use it

  • Prices differ by course. COMAP's bookstore lists the Course 2 and Course 3 print student editions at $125.00 each for 1–24 copies and $99.00 each for 25 or more. Courses 1 and 4 are listed as "Out of Stock" with "Email for pricing." The pages list class sets (25 student editions, a teacher's edition CD-ROM and a DVD) for Courses 1–3 but show no price for them, so ask COMAP.
  • COMAP calls the graphing calculator essential throughout the program. Computers are strongly recommended, though the materials let you teach the lessons without them. The teacher's edition for Courses 1–3 is sold on CD-ROM, the video on DVD, and the pages list CD-ROM, USB and web portal as format options.
  • The only preview we found is behind COMAP's questionnaire (name, school, address, email, course name, start date and number of students); we did not see any lessons.
  • The editions are dated: Course 1 is ©2010, Course 2 ©2011, Course 3 ©2013, and Course 4 is a ©2000 book. The pages say each course covers the content in the NCTM Standards; they don't mention later standards.
  • Lessons can run several days each. In the revised Course 1, many activities are designed for one 45-minute class period.

What a teacher gets

Mathematics: Modeling Our World is COMAP's integrated core curriculum for high school, the title under which the NSF-funded ARISE materials were sold. Real-world questions come first: in each unit students build, test and present models of a situation, such as deciding where to build a fire station.

Courses 1, 2 and 3 each have seven or eight units of four to seven lessons, and a lesson may take several days. Each lesson has a Lesson Opener that sets the context, Activities that students do in pairs or small groups, and Individual Work for practice and review. Course 4 has eight chapters (functions, exponential and logarithmic functions, polynomial models, vectors, matrices, analytic geometry, counting, and discrete dynamical systems) and is meant as a bridge to college mathematics. Course 1 has a unit on secret codes and algebra; Course 3 has units on apportionment, sampling and oscillation. For Courses 1–3, teacher materials include an Annotated Teacher's Edition, a Solutions Manual and Teacher's Resources with handouts and assessment problems; a video and a CD-ROM of calculator and computer programs go with the series.

How these awards connect to this product

NSF award 9255252 (Applications/Reform in Secondary Education (ARISE), Garfunkel PI, Godbold co-PI, 1992–1998) funded COMAP to "produce, field test, and evaluate a three year secondary school mathematics curriculum" with a 9–11 core. Award 9730430 (ARISE Course Four, Godbold PI, Garfunkel co-PI, 1998–2001) funded a fourth-year course that "completes the materials for a high school curriculum," and its abstract says "The entire series of instructional materials is being marketed under the title, Mathematics: Modeling Our World." The Course 4 book COMAP sells today is ©2000, within 9730430's dates (02/15/1998–01/31/2001), so it may be the NSF-funded edition itself; no source says so either way. Courses 1, 2 and 3 are sold as second editions (2010, 2011 and 2013); the Course 1 page says COMAP made "a major revision" based on users' feedback. No source we saw says who paid for the second editions, so we use "Funded an earlier edition" for the series as a whole. An NSF keyword search for "Modeling Our World" also returned 0137772 (COMPASS), a secondary mathematics implementation project whose abstract lists Mathematics: Modeling Our World among five programs; we did not treat it as product funding.
