Before you use it

  • The audience is teacher educators, researchers and teachers studying student thinking: the videos show researchers working with students, not ready-made lessons for students.
  • Viewing needs no account: on Oct 25, 2026 we searched the collection, and a clip's player (13 minutes, 32 seconds) and its transcript opened without logging in. Creating analytics requires joining the VMC Community by application, limited to people in education research, training and practice, and agreeing to a code of conduct.
  • The code of conduct says videos are for streaming only and may not be downloaded, or used commercially, without written permission from the collection owners, even though search results label the video links "Download MP4".
  • Footage dates from 1992 onward. The 2007 award that proposed the database planned to index episodes to NCTM's process standards for reasoning and justification.
  • Videos come with transcripts; time per video and suggested uses beyond the FAQ's general advice are not stated.

What a teacher gets

The Video Mosaic Collaborative is a Rutgers University Libraries portal to the Robert B. Davis Institute for Learning video collection: 300+ videos (100+ hours) from research projects that followed the same students from first grade through high school, starting in 1992. The collection mixes edited clips that highlight student reasoning and teaching practices with raw research footage. Videos can be searched by grade, mathematical strand (such as fractions, combinatorics or geometry), student name and form of reasoning; each has a description of the problem, how the researchers worked with students and the reasoning shown, and a transcript, sometimes with student work. Many clips are parts of a series.

The site also publishes "analytics," annotated sequences of clips that read like short multimedia articles, for example a three-part series tracing one student, Romina, from fourth grade to adulthood. Members of the VMC Community can build their own analytics with the RUAnalytic tool for courses and PD.

How these awards connect to this product

The Video Mosaic site credits NSF awards DRL-0822204 and IIS-1217087, and both NSF records describe work built on the Video Mosaic Collaborative. The record for 0723475 (Maher, 2007) describes a proof-of-concept, Internet-accessible database of analyzed video from the Rutgers longitudinal studies of students' reasoning, which matches the collection, but no source we saved names that award as VMC funding. The earlier longitudinal studies that produced the footage (for example 9053597 and 9814846 on the shortlist) are not listed here.
