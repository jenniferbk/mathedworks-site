  • Expect to teach through math workshop: a context, an investigation with conferring, a gallery walk and a whole-class Math Congress.

What a teacher gets

Contexts for Learning Mathematics (CFLM) is a K–5 curriculum by Catherine Fosnot built around math workshop. Each unit starts from a context children can imagine, moves into an investigation in which the teacher questions and confers, then has students prepare arguments, give peer feedback in a gallery walk and defend ideas in a whole-class Math Congress. Units include minilessons, number strings and games for fluency with basic facts. Learning is organized by "landscapes of learning" that map the big ideas, strategies and models for each grade.

The publisher now describes CFLM as a full core curriculum aligned to the Common Core and publishes a standards correlation for kindergarten through grade 5 that lists the units by name. Related products include a Dynamic Assessment System for documenting student strategies and an online video library for teacher professional learning.

How these awards connect to this product

Both awards went to Catherine Fosnot at City College. 9550080 (Summermath in the City) was a teacher enhancement project with the Freudenthal Institute that produced classroom video; 9911841 (Mathematics in the City) developed K–8 inservice materials on number and operation expected to be published by Heinemann. Heinemann says Mathematics in the City was funded by NSF and ExxonMobil, and New Perspectives on Learning calls its classroom video library NSF-funded. No source we saved says NSF funded the Contexts for Learning Mathematics units themselves, so the connection is recorded as inferred.
