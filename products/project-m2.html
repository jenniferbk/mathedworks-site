Project M2: Mentoring Young Mathematicians

Eight inquiry-based K–2 units on geometry, measurement and number, with character-driven investigations, student journals and manipulative kits, sold by Kendall Hunt.

Before you use it

  • Cost for one unit (Level 1 Unit 1, Kendall Hunt, Oct 25, 2026): Teacher Guide Package $157.00, Teacher Resource Package (hint, think-beyond and word-wall cards, sold separately) $52.50, manipulative kit $281.00 and Student Mathematician Journals at $13.00 each, about $750 for a class of 20. Other units are listed as separate products.
  • Try a free sample lesson through Kendall Hunt's form, which asks for your name, state and email.
  • These are replacement units, not a full-year curriculum: two units in kindergarten and three each in grades 1 and 2. Lesson counts and lesson length aren't stated; the NSF proposal planned about 4–5 lessons per unit.
  • Prep: the Level 1 Unit 1 kit includes counters, dice, pattern blocks, tangrams, mirrors, stopwatches, straws, twine and project card sets, so plan storage and setup. Ebook licenses are time-limited (three years for the teacher guide, one year for student journals). Some listed trade books are unavailable.
  • Age and standards: the Level 1 Unit 1 student journal is copyright 2016 (we did not check dates for the other units), and the units are aligned to the Common Core; the Level 1 correlation lists geometry, measurement and data standards from kindergarten through grade 4, mostly grade 1. Spanish editions, planned in the NSF proposal, aren't mentioned by the publisher.

Try one lesson

No free lesson to look at. Kendall Hunt's free sample lesson downloads only after a form asking for name, state and email, and trial requests go to a separate form; we found no lesson that opens without them.

What a teacher gets

Project M2 is a series of eight units for kindergarten through grade 2: geometry and measurement units at each level (for example, Exploring Shapes in Space in kindergarten, Creating the School Measurement Fair in grade 1, Designing a Shape Gallery in grade 2), plus number units for Levels 1 and 2, the grade 2 unit covering expressions, equations and the equal sign. Characters guide students through investigations that stress reasoning and mathematical communication in discussion and writing. NSF describes the geometry and measurement units as advanced but differentiated for a range of students.

Per unit, a teacher buys a Teacher Guide Package (print guide with a three-year ebook license and a Scrapbook of short read-along vignettes; Level K adds a Center Book) and a manipulative kit, plus, for six of eight listed units, a separate Teacher Resource Package (hint, "think beyond" and word-wall cards); each student needs a Student Mathematician Journal (96 pages in Level 1 Unit 1). The teacher guide includes unit overviews, materials lists, background on the math, and rubrics. NSF funded the six geometry and measurement units.

How these awards connect to this product

NSF award 0733189 (Gavin, PI; Casa, co-PI) funded the development and field testing of the geometry and measurement units. Its outcomes report says the project produced six units, one geometry and one measurement unit at each of kindergarten, grade 1 and grade 2. Kendall Hunt now sells eight units; the two number units for Levels 1 and 2 are not described in the NSF record, and no source we saved says how they were funded. Kendall Hunt's author pages describe Project M2 as an NSF research grant.
