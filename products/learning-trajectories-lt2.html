Before you use it

  • Free. Some content is for registered users only; you can sign up for a free account.

What a teacher gets

[LT]2 is a website from the Marsico Institute at the University of Denver, led by Douglas Clements and Julie Sarama, for anyone supporting young children's early math, birth to age 8. Each learning trajectory has three parts: a goal, a developmental progression of levels children typically pass through, and instructional activities matched to each level. Trajectories on the site include counting, measurement of length, and patterns, structure and algebraic thinking.

Each level page describes what a child at that level can do; the sample level we read (Counter (10)) also has practice notes and a link to activities. An Explore tool lets you navigate by math strand, age or grade, assessments such as Teaching Strategies GOLD, and standards from the Common Core, Head Start and various states. A teachers page links a description of all trajectories, practice-guide videos, and handouts, some in Spanish. The resources page has printable numeral cards, dot cards and tangram puzzles. Registered teachers can set up classes and student logins so children can play the site's games.

How these awards connect to this product

The site does not name an NSF award in the pages we saw; its About page says the research and continued development of this free resource are funded by research grants and supporters of early learning, and its teachers page credits a partnership with the Institute of Education Sciences and the Regional Education Lab for the practice guides and videos it links. NSF award 1908889 (Clements, Sarama, Ready; 2019–2027) is an efficacy study of "the Learning and Teaching with Learning Trajectories (LT2) program", which its record describes as a professional development tool and curriculum resource; the record tests the program and does not say NSF funded building the site. Award 2300606 (the Building Blocks Toolset app) builds on the same trajectory research but does not name [LT]2, so it is not listed.
