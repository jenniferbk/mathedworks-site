Before you use it

  • The games, directions and printables are posted as freely available and open without a login. The boxed kits are sold separately by NewPath Learning; the complete program set was listed "Starting at $349.95."
  • You'll need to print and cut out card decks or game boards, and gather simple materials such as dice, counters or dot cards, depending on the game.
  • The site says its games are designed for children ages 3 to 6 and doesn't name grade levels; individual direction sheets give their own ages (Roll Two 1–6 says 4+).
  • The site says its games "can be played in just a few minutes" (Roll Two 1–6 says 5–10 minutes). They are short games, not a full curriculum.
  • The site footer reads "Copyright © 2025 Education Development Center, Inc. All Rights Reserved" and says the work is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike, which lets you share and adapt for noncommercial use with credit, sharing adaptations under the same license. Try one lesson

Roll Two 1 to 6 youngmathematicians.edc.org

  • Ages 4+
  • Matching and combining numbers rolled on dice; counting on
  • 5–10 min
  • Free, no login

Each child puts a set of 1–6 cards in order, then players take turns rolling two dice with 1–3 dots and turn over cards that match one rolled number, both numbers, or their sum, until all their cards are turned over.

You need: Two dice with 1–3 dots and a printed set of 1–6 Count and See Cards for each player; for 1–4 players

The game page links four direction-sheet PDFs (we read the English one; the file names indicate Spanish, Portuguese and Haitian Creole versions), printable 0–12 cards and optional game boards.

What a teacher gets

Young Mathematicians is an EDC project that built math games for young children with teachers and families, to be played at school and at home. The site offers over 55 freely available games for children ages 3 to 6, with printable materials, directions, and videos in English, Spanish and Portuguese.

Games are grouped into sets such as Dot Card Games, Games with Cards and Dice, Games with Fingers and Counters, Movement Games, Pattern and Sorting Games, and Shape Games and Puzzles, and the site can also be browsed by topic, from counting and cardinality and adding and subtracting to shapes, spatial relations, measurement, and data collection. Some games are quick and use everyday materials; others use cards or a game board for longer play, and each can be made easier or harder. A game page gives the goal, the materials, direction sheets and printable cards or boards. The site also has math minibooks and a Math Picture Books section. Boxed game kits with teacher guides are sold separately through NewPath Learning.

How these awards connect to this product

The Young Mathematicians site credits "the National Science Foundation Grants DUE1348564, DRL 1907904" alongside seven Heising-Simons Foundation grants and an Overdeck Family Foundation grant. Both NSF awards are confirmed in the API (PI Jessica M Young, Education Development Center). The outcomes report for 1348564 (2014-2019) says the Young Mathematicians study "developed games as challenging problem-solving activities" for Head Start classrooms, and the outcomes report for 1907904 (2019-2024) says Phase 1 "led to the development and refinement of over 55 early math games with teacher and family instructional supports." Because the site credits private foundations too and no source says which games each funder paid for, we use "Funded part of this product."
